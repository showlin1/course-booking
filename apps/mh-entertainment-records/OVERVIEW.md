# MH Entertainment Records

Premium landing page for MH Entertainment Records — an independent French record label (Maison de Disque) based in Paris.

**Audience:** Artists, labels, music industry professionals, and the general public looking to discover MH Entertainment's services and submit music projects.

**Purpose:** Showcase MH Entertainment as a professional, internationally-minded record label. Provide clear pathways for artists to submit projects (label, radio single, TV music video), display news/updates, list industry partners, and present the label's expertises in music promotion, creation, and communication.

**Key sections:** Hero, La Maison (about), Expertises, Global Power (featured video), Submit Project (3 submission paths), Radio/TV submissions, International vision, News/Journal, Partners/Media industry, CTA, and Footer.
