# Kanova Life – Course Booking

Public website for Kanova Life (Canadian Red Cross Training Partner, Thunder Bay). Visitors browse the first aid / CPR courses, view course details, then book a session: they pick a location (Thunder Bay, Nipigon, Terrace Bay and their community centres), choose an available date within the course's schedule window on a calendar, and enter their details. Bookings are saved for the team to review. Course prices and schedule dates are managed in the Courses table.
